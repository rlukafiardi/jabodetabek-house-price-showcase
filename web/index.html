About this map and estimate

The map shows the median asking price per m² of land, from about 4,000 house listings of an Indonesian property portal (). A price is shown only where an area has at least 5 listings. Colours are equal-count classes, so outer areas stay readable next to central Jakarta. An outlined kecamatan mixes premium and ordinary pockets, so its median is not one typical price. No listing-level data is published.

The estimate is the mean of three models on location, size, rooms, nearby transport, road and land-use context, and the notes. It predicts an asking price, not a sale price or a valuation. On listings the model never saw, half of the estimates were within of the asking price; the likely range held of them and the possible range . Plots over 300 m² are harder to price and get wider ranges. Remote kecamatan have few listings, so estimates there are less certain.
